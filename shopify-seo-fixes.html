Why Your Shopify Store Isn't Ranking

Many Shopify stores have technical SEO issues that prevent Google from properly understanding and ranking their pages. These aren't content problems - they're structural issues that need fixing at the code level.

Common issues include missing or duplicate meta tags, improper use of heading tags, missing structured data, broken internal links, and incorrect sitemap configuration.

What We Fix

Meta Tags

Title tags, meta descriptions, and Open Graph tags optimized for each page type.

Structured Data

Product schema, organization schema, and breadcrumbs for rich search results.

Technical Issues

Sitemap errors, robots.txt problems, canonical URLs, and indexing issues.

Site Structure

Heading hierarchy, internal linking, and URL structure optimization.

Our SEO Fix Process

1. Technical Audit

We crawl your store like Google does, identifying every technical SEO issue. You'll receive a detailed report showing exactly what needs fixing.

2. Priority Ranking

Not all SEO issues are equal. We prioritize fixes that will have the biggest impact on your search visibility first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't my Shopify store showing up on Google?

Common reasons include missing or poor meta tags, duplicate content issues, incorrect robots.txt settings, missing sitemap, or technical SEO problems. We'll audit your store and fix what's preventing Google from properly indexing it.

How long until I see SEO improvements?

Technical SEO fixes can show results within 2-4 weeks as Google recrawls your site. However, ranking improvements depend on many factors including competition and content quality. We fix the technical foundation that allows your store to rank.

Do you write content or just fix technical SEO?

We focus on technical SEO fixes - the things that prevent Google from properly understanding and ranking your store. This includes meta tags, structured data, sitemaps, and fixing technical errors. We don't write blog posts or product descriptions.

What's included in an SEO audit?

We check meta tags on all pages, structured data implementation, sitemap and robots.txt configuration, duplicate content issues, page speed (which affects SEO), mobile-friendliness, and internal linking structure.
