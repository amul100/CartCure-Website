Why Speed Matters for Your Shopify Store

Every second counts in ecommerce. Studies show that a 1-second delay in page load time can result in a 7% reduction in conversions. For a store making $10,000/month, that's $700 in lost sales.

Google also uses page speed as a ranking factor, meaning slow stores appear lower in search results. Faster stores get more organic traffic and convert more visitors into customers.

What We Optimize

Image Optimization

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Shopify store so slow?

Common causes include unoptimized images, too many apps, heavy theme code, excessive JavaScript, and third-party scripts. We'll identify the specific issues slowing down your store and fix them.

How much faster will my store be?

Results vary depending on your store's current state, but most stores see 30-60% improvement in load times. We focus on the changes that will have the biggest impact on your specific store.

Will speed optimization break anything on my store?

We always create a backup before making changes and test thoroughly. Our optimizations are carefully implemented to maintain all your store's functionality while improving performance.

How long does Shopify speed optimization take?

Most speed optimization projects are completed within 3-7 days, depending on the complexity. We'll provide a specific timeline when we quote your project.
