Frequently Asked Questions

Why are customers abandoning their carts?

Common reasons include unexpected shipping costs, complicated checkout process, lack of payment options, trust concerns, or technical issues. We'll help identify what's causing abandonment on your specific store and fix it.

Can you customize Shopify checkout?

Shopify limits checkout customization on most plans. On Shopify Plus you have full control. On other plans, we can customize the cart page, add trust badges, optimize shipping and payment settings, and improve the pre-checkout experience.

What checkout improvements typically help most?

The biggest improvements usually come from adding more payment options (like Afterpay), displaying shipping costs earlier, adding trust signals, simplifying the cart page, and setting up abandoned cart recovery emails.

How much can checkout optimization improve sales?

Results vary, but stores typically see 10-30% improvement in checkout completion rates after optimization. Even small improvements can have significant impact - reducing cart abandonment from 70% to 60% means 33% more completed orders.
