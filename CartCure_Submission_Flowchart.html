Job has been created. No further actions available on submission.

  • ✓ Job # assigned (J-prefix)
  • ✓ Client details copied to job

Invoice Types Explained

💰 Full Invoice (100%)

Used for small projects under $200. Single payment covers entire job.

  • ✓ Quote Amount: < $200
  • ✓ Generated when: Job accepted or completed

💵 Deposit Invoice (50%)

First invoice for larger projects. Secures commitment before work starts.

  • ✓ Quote Amount: ≥ $200
  • ✓ Auto-generated on quote acceptance
  • ✓ Must be paid before work starts

💳 Balance Invoice (50%)

Final invoice after deposit paid. Covers remaining project cost.

  • ✓ Generated after deposit marked Paid
  • ✓ Usually sent on job completion
  • ✓ Amount = Total - Deposit Paid

➕ Additional Invoice

For any remaining balance after partial payments or scope changes.

  • ✓ Manually triggered
  • ✓ Amount = Total - All Paid Invoices

Automated Processes & Triggers

Daily Quote Reminders (9 AM NZ)

Function: autoSendQuoteReminders()

Condition: Job status = "Quoted" AND quote sent 7+ days ago

Action: Sends reminder email, changes status to "Quote Reminded"

Daily Invoice Reminders (9 AM NZ)

Function: autoSendInvoiceReminders()

Condition: Invoice status = "Sent" AND due date 1-2 days away

Action: Sends friendly reminder email with "I Have Paid" button

Daily Overdue Processing (9 AM NZ)

Function: autoSendOverdueInvoices()

Condition: Invoice past due date AND (Days Overdue % 7 = 1)

Action: Updates to "Overdue", calculates late fees (2%/day), sends overdue notice weekly

Background Task Queue

Function: processBackgroundTasks()

Trigger: Multi-step operations (e.g., quote acceptance)

Action: Processes queued tasks with retry logic (up to 3 attempts)
